The Problem

You can't hear your sales floor.
So you coach on guesses.

🏬

One manager, many conversations

You might catch a handful of pitches a week. The rest happen while you're at another store, on the phone, or writing up a deal. Those conversations decide your close rate, and nobody hears them.

🧭

The process lives on paper

Every dealer has a sales process. Few know who actually follows it. Reps drift toward price and product specs, skip the profile build, and never ask for the sale. You only see it in the numbers, weeks later.

🚪

Walk-outs disappear

Most showroom customers don't buy on the first visit. Follow-up depends on a rep remembering, with nothing written down about what the customer wanted or what was quoted. Those are your best leads, and they leak.

How It Works

From the first hello to the follow-up

Reps change one habit: tap Record before the walk-up. Noetra does the rest.

01

Record

The rep taps Record on the Noetra app before greeting a customer. Phone in a pocket or on the counter. No hardware, no wiring, works in any showroom.

02

Score & coach

Noetra transcribes the conversation, separates rep from customer, and scores each step of your sales process 0 to 5 for an overall 0 to 100. The rep gets written coaching on that exact pitch.

03

Capture the outcome

After the pitch, a one-minute form: who the customer was, what they were interested in, what was quoted, whether they bought, and when to follow up. Picked from your own product list, not typed.

04

Follow up & manage

The recording, score and form land in your CRM, so follow-up starts without anyone remembering to do it. Managers see every store, every rep and every pitch on one dashboard.
