Why this exists

You can't be on the floor for every conversation. So most of them are never coached.

A sales manager at a busy store hears maybe five or six pitches a week. The other forty happen while they're at another location, on the phone with a vendor, or desking a deal in the back. Those forty decide the close rate, and nobody knows what was said in them.

The usual fix is a month-end report and a hunch. The report tells you who sold what. It doesn't tell you that one rep skips the profile build every time, another never asks for the sale, and a third is quoting before the customer has sat in the tub or driven the truck.

Noetra fixes the part that was missing: the conversation itself, recorded, scored and handed back to the rep and the manager the same day.

"Before Noetra I had to pick which store to be at. Now I can hear every pitch, at every showroom, and coach my team on specifics instead of guesses." Sales manager, Hot Tub Superstore
What comes back

One real conversation, thirty-eight minutes on a showroom floor.

This is an actual recording from one of our stores, with the rep's name removed. Not long after she tapped Stop, this is what she and her manager were looking at.

The transcript
Rep and customer separated into turns, so you can read exactly where the objection came up and what was said back.
Six scores
Greeting, profile build, presentation, demonstration, write-up and close, each 0 to 5, rolled into an overall score out of 100. This one scored 73.
The coaching
What went well, what to fix, and the exact question to ask next time. Here it was "When are you looking to have the hot tub installed?", which the rep never asked.
The outcome
A one-minute form after the pitch: budget range, what they were interested in, whether a proposal was given, whether they bought, and the next get-together. That form is what starts the follow-up in the CRM.
How it works

Reps change one habit. Everything else is automatic.

  1. Tap Record

    Before the greeting, the rep taps Record on the Noetra app. Phone in a pocket, on the counter, or along on the walk-around. Nothing to install in the building.

  2. Noetra listens

    The conversation is transcribed with rep and customer separated, then scored step by step against your sales process. Written coaching comes back to the rep and the manager.

  3. The rep logs the outcome

    Who the customer was, what they wanted, what was quoted, whether they bought, when to follow up. Picked from your product list, not typed.

  4. Follow-up starts

    The recording, score and outcome land in your CRM. If you don't have one, we set you up on Noetra CRM. Managers see every store and every rep in one place.

Questions dealers ask first
What does it cost?

It depends on how many stores and reps you have and whether you need Noetra CRM or already have a CRM we connect to. We quote it on the intro call, once we understand your setup. There's no pricing page because we'd rather give you a real number than a tier name.

What about recording consent?

Laws vary by state. Our stores post a notice at the entrance and reps mention it where required. We'll share what we've seen work during onboarding, but compliance in your state is your responsibility and worth a call to your attorney.

How long until my team is recording?

Setup takes a few days after the intro call. On the team launch call every rep installs the app and records the same day. The first month is spent tuning the scoring to your process and building the habit.

Who can see the recordings?

Reps see their own. Managers see their stores. The Noetra team that supports your account can see them to help you. Recordings belong to your dealership and aren't shared with other dealers.

We have several stores. Does one manager see all of them?

Yes. Each store is its own account under your group, with its own time zone, product list and targets. Managers switch between stores or look at the group as a whole.
