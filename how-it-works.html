How it works

From the first hello to the follow-up call.

Noetra starts with a sales process and the training to put it on your floor. The software is what keeps it there: there's no hardware, no wiring and nothing for the customer to see. A rep carries the phone they already have. Here is what happens on a normal afternoon.

1. Record

The rep taps Record before the greeting.

On a showroom floor the phone sits in a shirt pocket or on the counter. On a car lot it comes along on the walk-around and the demo drive. When the customer leaves, the rep taps Stop and the recording uploads on its own.

Reps can also record a phone call or a quick practice pitch the same way. Every recording is attributed to the rep and the store, so a manager with three locations sees three locations.

2. Score and coach

Each step of the Noetra process gets a grade, and the rep gets told why.

Noetra transcribes the conversation and separates the rep from the customer. Then it scores each step of the Noetra sales process from 0 to 5: greeting, profile build, presentation, demonstration, write-up and close. The process is the same in every showroom. What we customize for each dealer are the fine points inside each step: your products, your write-up, the questions that matter on your floor, and the names you use for the steps.

The scores roll up to an overall number out of 100. Alongside it comes written coaching: what the rep did well, what they skipped, and the specific question or line to use next time. Both the rep and the manager get it, on the phone and on the web.

3. Log the outcome

One minute after the pitch: what happened?

The form asks who the customer was, what they were interested in, what was quoted, whether they bought, and when the next contact should be. The choices come from your own product list, so a hot tub store picks brands and cover types and a dealership picks the vehicle, the trade and the financing path.

If a rep forgets, the app reminds them the next morning. Managers can see which recordings are still missing a form.

4. Follow up and manage

The recording, the score and the outcome land in your CRM.

Every scored recording and every outcome form is pushed to your CRM the moment it's ready. On GoHighLevel that means the contact is created or updated, the pitch summary is on the record, and whatever follow-up sequence you've set starts without anyone remembering to do it. Other CRMs connect by webhook.

Dealers without a CRM get Noetra CRM: contacts, a pipeline for open deals, automated text and email follow-up, and a booking calendar.

Managers get a dashboard per store and per rep: recordings this week against the target, average score, which steps are slipping, and an alert when someone stops recording.

The training

The Noetra Method

Scoring a conversation only helps if the rep knows what a five looks like. So every team is trained first. The Noetra Method is a ten-module sales course built on one idea: the customer is standing in your store for an emotional reason, and the sale is finding that reason and selling to it. Greeting, profile build, presentation, demonstration, write-up, close and follow-up, each with the exact rubric the app scores against, each fitted to your products and your floor. Every module ends with a recorded drill that Noetra grades, so training and coaching use the same yardstick from day one.

New hires take the same course later. Managers can see who has done which module.
