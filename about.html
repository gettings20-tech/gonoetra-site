About

We sell hot tubs and used cars. Noetra is how we coach our own floors.

Noetra is a small company in Henderson, Nevada. It exists because its founder owns five stores and couldn't be on any of their sales floors most of the day.

I'm Scott Gettings. I own Hot Tub Superstore, which is three showrooms in Reno, Henderson and Scottsdale, and Eagle Valley Motors, two car lots in Carson City and Reno.

For a long time my sales coaching looked like everyone else's: the month-end report, the occasional pitch I happened to overhear, and a feeling about who was good. The report told me who sold. It never told me why two reps standing in the same building, selling the same tubs, closed so differently.

So we started recording. In the first month we scored more than sixty conversations across the three showrooms, and I learned more about my sales floor than I had in years. Some reps were strong at the greeting and weak at the close. Some were the opposite. A few were better than I'd given them credit for. None of it was visible in the report.

That's what we install for other dealers now. Not software you log into and figure out, but the setup we run ourselves, put in by the people who run it, with our own sales course for your team and a CRM if you don't have one.

Scott GettingsFounder, Noetra · Owner, Hot Tub Superstore and Eagle Valley Motors
How we work with you

Four steps from the first call to a floor that records every pitch.

  1. Intro call

    Thirty minutes. You tell us about your stores and how you sell. We show Noetra on real recordings from our own floors and tell you honestly whether it fits.

  2. Setup

    We build your account: stores, reps, your sales steps, the products and brands your reps will pick from, and your CRM connection or Noetra CRM.

  3. Team launch

    One call with every rep on their phone. The app goes on, the Noetra Method starts, and the first recordings happen that day.

  4. First thirty days

    We listen alongside you, tune the scoring to your language, and set recording targets per rep. By day thirty you're coaching from what was said.

Plans are based on the number of stores and reps and whether you need Noetra CRM. We quote on the intro call. There is no pricing page because the honest answer is "it depends on your stores," and we'd rather say that to you than hide it behind a tier name.

The people

A dealer, a sales trainer, and a CRM team.

Scott runs the stores and the product. The onboarding and the Noetra Method come from the same people who train our own reps. The CRM side, including Noetra CRM for dealers who need one, is run by a team that builds dealer follow-up systems for a living.

When you book a call, you're talking to someone who has stood on a sales floor this week.
