The problem on a lot

The CRM knows the up came in. It doesn't know what was said.

A customer spends forty-five minutes with a salesperson: the greeting, the needs questions, the walk-around, the demo drive, the write-up. Then they go home to think about it, and the only record of the visit is a CRM note that says "looked at the F-150, following up Tuesday."

The manager finds out weeks later, from the close rate, that one salesperson quotes payments before the demo drive, another never asks about the trade, and the newest hire skips the walk-around entirely when it's hot out.

Noetra puts the conversation itself in front of the manager and the salesperson the same afternoon, scored against the road-to-the-sale you already train.

Your steps, scored
Meet and greet, needs discovery, vehicle selection, walk-around, demo drive, trade, write-up, close. Or whatever your road-to-the-sale calls them. Each 0 to 5, with written coaching.
Outcome form
Vehicle shown, stock number, trade discussed, financing path, numbers presented, bought or not, and when to follow up. Picked from lists, filled in under a minute.
CRM follow-up
Summary and outcome pushed to your CRM so the follow-up sequence starts on its own. No CRM? Noetra CRM is set up for you.
Multi-rooftop
Each store is its own account under the group. A GM or owner sees every rooftop; a sales manager sees their store.
For the sales manager

Coach the one conversation that matters this week, not the whole month.

Every salesperson has a page: how many pitches they recorded against their target, their average score, and which step is dragging it down. When a 55 shows up on a Saturday, the manager listens to that one, reads the coaching, and has a five-minute conversation Monday morning with the transcript open.

The weekly recording target does the nagging for you. Reps who stop recording show up as alerts, not as a surprise at month end.

Dealership questions
Does it work with our DMS or CRM?

Noetra connects to GoHighLevel directly and to other CRMs by webhook, which most modern dealer CRMs accept. We'll confirm yours on the intro call. It doesn't touch your DMS; the outcome form captures the vehicle and the numbers without needing to.

What about recording on the demo drive?

The phone records in a pocket or a cup holder. Road noise is fine. The scoring looks at what was said, not audio quality.

Do we have to change our road-to-the-sale?

No. We load your steps and your language. If you don't have a written process, we start you on ours and adjust it over the first month.

What does it cost?

It depends on rooftops, salespeople and whether you need Noetra CRM. We quote on the intro call.
