For showroom retailers

Most of your customers leave to think about it. Know exactly what they heard.

Hot tubs, swim spas, saunas, furniture, appliances, kitchens: a high-ticket showroom sale is a long conversation and a customer who usually walks out the first time. Noetra records that conversation, scores it, coaches the rep, and makes sure the walk-out gets followed up.

We run three of these

Built on a hot tub floor, by the people selling on it.

Hot Tub Superstore has showrooms in Reno, Henderson and Scottsdale. One sales manager, five reps, three buildings in two states. For years the manager coached whichever store they were standing in, and the other two sold unsupervised.

Noetra started as the tool we built to fix that. The six steps it scores by default are the six steps our reps are trained on: greeting, profile build, presentation, demonstration, write-up and close. The outcome form asks the questions our managers ask after every up: budget range, what they were interested in, which brands, proposal given, bought or not, next get-together.

If you sell something other than hot tubs, the steps and the product list change. The shape of the sale doesn't.

For the sales manager

Every store, every rep, every pitch, from wherever you are.

The dashboard shows recordings this week against each rep's target, the team's average score, and who is improving or slipping. Click a rep and you see the step that's costing them: the one who greets at a five and closes at a two, the one who presents before they've asked a single profile question.

Multi-store groups get a store switcher. Each location keeps its own time zone, product list and targets; the owner sees the group.
